House rules

Read before Friday
  • 01

    One piece per person

    Matched on card, address and device. Second orders are refunded, not shipped — and the unit goes back into the queue.

  • 02

    The queue is random

    Position is assigned by lottery when the timer hits zero, not by who refreshed hardest. Joining early does nothing except reserve your entry.

  • 03

    Nothing is ever restocked

    The dies are destroyed after each run. Whatever number you get is the only one of it that will ever exist.

  • 04

    Ten minutes to check out

    Your slot holds for ten minutes. After that it releases to the next person in line, automatically and without appeal.
