/ Process

From carbon seed
to cold finish

STEP 01

Seed

A 0.3 mm diamond sliver enters the reactor. Methane plasma deposits carbon atom by atom across four weeks.

STEP 02

Cut

Mapped by laser tomography, then cut to a 57-facet brilliant at tolerances measured in hundredths of a degree.

STEP 03

Grade

Spectrometry, fluorescence and clarity scoring. Below VVS2 it goes back to the chamber, not to the shelf.

STEP 04

Set

Seated into platinum or solid 18K, finished in a −42°C bath that tightens the metal around each stone.
