How to read this page

The per-gram column is the honest one

A plain 14K chain runs about $24 a gram here. That covers the metal at the day's fix, the labour, and our margin. When a piece costs more per gram, it's because stones or setting hours went into it — never because the chain got heavier.

Weight is taken after polishing

Polishing removes metal. A lot of shops weigh before, quote you that number, and hand you something lighter. Every figure here is the scale reading from the finished piece, and we'll re-weigh it in front of you before you pay.

"One only" means the dies are gone

Where an entry says one only, it was a single commission we bought back or a one-off cast. When it sells we won't make another. Everything else we can reorder in four to six weeks at the same per-gram rate.

ICE JEWELS

Ice Jewels keeps a bench and a counter at one address and sells nothing it has not weighed. Metal is bought at the London fix and the day's rate is written on the tag. Stones are lab-grown unless the entry above says mined, and where they are mined the supplier's name is on the receipt. Repairs are free for as long as we are open, which includes resizing, re-tipping, re-stringing, re-polishing, and replacing a stone we set. We do not charge for appraisal, we do not sell plated goods, and we do not price a piece by what we think you can pay. Written enquiries to counter@icejewels.com; the bench answers its own mail. Trade-in terms and the current gold fix are posted on the rates page.
