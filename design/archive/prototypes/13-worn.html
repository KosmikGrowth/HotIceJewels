The ones that weren't kind

A testimonial page that only carries praise is an advertisement. These are the complaints, unedited, with what we said back. We have not removed the ones where the customer was right.

  • "Six weeks is six weeks. Other places said 48 hours and I didn't believe the timeline until I'd already paid a deposit here."

    Tomas Iversen · Commission

    We said: fair. We now put the timeline on the quote in writing before the deposit, not after, because of this.

  • "The shop is tiny and there's nowhere to sit while you wait. Felt rushed on a Saturday."

    Nadia Hallgren · Walk-in

    We said: also fair, and unfixed. The bench takes most of the room and we would rather keep the bench.

  • "Told me I didn't need VVS. I wanted VVS. It came across like they were deciding what I could have."

    Ben Achebe · Owner

    We said: a real mistake on our part. Advice once, then it's your call — we sold the VVS and we should have sooner.
