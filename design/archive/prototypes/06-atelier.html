Commissioned in four movements

The workshop takes twelve commissions at a time. Nothing is cast until the drawing is signed by both of us.

The drawing

An hour at the bench, in person or by video, ending in a signed graphite rendering.

The stones

You choose from loose stock under the scope. Nothing is committed until you have held them.

The bench

Cast, filed, set and polished in-house across four to nine weeks by one named maker.

The delivery

Hand-delivered in the city, or couriered insured, with the original drawing framed.

Eleven people.
One room.

We are not a brand with a workshop attached. We are a workshop that answers its own phone. The person who draws your piece is the person who sets it, and their initials go in beside yours.

No outsourcing, no white-label casting, no third-party setters. It is slower and it costs more, and it is the only way we know how to do it.
