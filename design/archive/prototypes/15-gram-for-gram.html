Why the margin is a fixed figure

If margin were a percentage, a heavier chain would earn us more for identical work. It takes the same bench hours to finish a 142 gram chain as a 380 gram one, so the number is flat and the same on every piece.

It also means the heavier you go, the closer your price per gram sits to the metal cost — which is the opposite of how most of the trade prices.
