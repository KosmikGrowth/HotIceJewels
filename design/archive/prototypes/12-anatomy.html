    Six words the trade uses to mean something other than what you think

    gold-filled
    Not filled with gold. A thin gold layer bonded to base metal — better than plating, still not solid.
    vermeil
    Gold plating over sterling silver. Legally defined, frequently used to sound like solid gold.
    eye-clean
    You cannot see the flaw without magnification. It says nothing about the actual clarity grade.
    carat / karat
    Two different words. Carat is stone weight; karat is gold purity. Shops blur them freely.
    cluster
    Many small stones arranged to read as one large one. Legitimate, but priced as if it were not.
    appraisal value
    An insurance replacement figure, always above retail. It is not what anyone will pay you.
