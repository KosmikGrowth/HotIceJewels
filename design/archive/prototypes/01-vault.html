Sourcing

Stones are bought loose in Antwerp, graded in-house, and rejected at a rate of roughly nine in ten. Nothing below VVS enters the workshop.

02

Casting

Solid 18K, lost-wax cast in single pours. No hollow bodies, no plating, no filler. A piece that weighs nothing is worth nothing.

03

Setting

Each stone is seated by hand under a scope. A single pendant takes one setter eleven days and is signed on the reverse.
