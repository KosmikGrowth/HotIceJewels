Our story

The cooliest thing you'll own.

Hot Ice Jewels makes diamond rings and earrings that feel cold, clear and calm, the way real ice does. Forty-five years of experience with diamonds goes into every piece.

Heritage

Forty-five years with diamonds

We have spent 45 years with diamonds: sorting them, setting them, and learning what makes one look alive. That experience is why we choose every stone ourselves, so you get the best diamonds.

Every design starts as a precise 3D file. The images on this site are rendered from those same files, so the piece you turn in 360° is the piece you get.

Lab-grown diamonds

Grown, not mined. Still a diamond.

A lab-grown diamond is made of the same pure carbon crystal as a mined diamond. It has the same hardness, the same fire and the same sparkle, and it is graded on the same four Cs. What changes is where it began: in a lab, under heat and pressure, instead of deep underground.

Gold

14k or 18k, solid all the way through

14k is 58.5% gold. It is harder, so it suits pieces you wear every day. 18k is 75% gold, with a deeper colour.

Every design comes in yellow, white and rose gold.
