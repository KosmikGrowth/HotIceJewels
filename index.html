Questions

What you need to know

Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. A lab-grown diamond is pure carbon crystal, the same material as a mined diamond, with the same hardness, fire and sparkle. Only its origin is different: it grows in a lab instead of deep underground.

How are lab-grown diamonds graded?

On the same four Cs as mined diamonds: cut, colour, clarity and carat. Independent labs such as IGI and GIA grade lab-grown diamonds the same way they grade mined ones.

What is the difference between 14k and 18k?

14k is 58.5% gold and 18k is 75% gold. 14k is harder, which suits pieces you wear every day; 18k has a richer colour. Both are solid gold all the way through.

Where do you ship?

Across India and to other countries.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Yes. Every order comes with a GST invoice.

How do I clean my diamond earrings?

Soak them for a few minutes in warm water with a drop of mild soap, brush gently behind the stones with a soft toothbrush, rinse, and dry with a lint-free cloth.
