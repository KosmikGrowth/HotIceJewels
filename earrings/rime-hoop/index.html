Lab-grown diamonds

Pure carbon crystal, the same as a mined diamond, graded on the same four Cs: cut, colour, clarity and carat. Lab-grown or mined?

14k or 18k

14k is 58.5% gold: harder, made for every day. 18k is 75% gold, with a richer colour. Both are solid all the way through. Which to choose
